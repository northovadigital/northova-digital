# Pho Ben Sugar Land — 3-Point Website Improvement Plan

## 1. Improve the Mobile Customer Journey

Create a cleaner mobile-first layout with simple navigation so customers can quickly access the menu, online ordering, catering, location, and contact information.

## 2. Organize Menu and Ordering Access

Replace scattered or external menu links with a structured branded menu and clearly visible ordering buttons for available delivery and pickup platforms.

## 3. Strengthen Catering and Contact Conversions

Create dedicated catering and contact sections with clear calls to action, simple inquiry forms, business information, and direct phone and email access.

## Expected Business Impact

- Faster access to menus and ordering
- Better experience for mobile customers
- More catering and customer inquiries
- Stronger professional brand presence
