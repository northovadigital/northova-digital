# Taste Kitchen + Bar — 3-Point Website Improvement Plan

## 1. Simplify the Homepage Journey

Refine the homepage structure so customers can reach the menu, reservations, locations, and other important actions without scrolling through excessive content.

## 2. Improve Menu Organization

Organize menu content into clearer categories with easier navigation, especially for mobile visitors exploring different dishes and dining options.

## 3. Strengthen Reviews and Social Proof

Feature customer reviews, ratings, media mentions, and other trust signals more prominently to reinforce the restaurant’s premium brand and encourage new customers to visit.

## Expected Business Impact

- Faster access to important customer actions
- Easier menu exploration on mobile devices
- Stronger trust with first-time visitors
- More focused and conversion-friendly homepage experience
