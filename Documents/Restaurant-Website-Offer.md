# Northova Digital — Restaurant Website Package

## Service

A modern, mobile-friendly restaurant website designed to help restaurants attract customers, receive reservations and promote online ordering.

## Included

- Modern responsive website
- Home and restaurant story sections
- Featured menu with prices
- Online ordering links
- Reservation request form
- Food and restaurant gallery
- Customer testimonials
- Google Maps integration
- Click-to-call and email links
- Opening hours and contact details
- Social media links
- Basic search engine optimization
- Website deployment and launch

## Client Provides

- Restaurant name and logo
- Menu and pricing
- Food and restaurant images
- Address and opening hours
- Phone number and email
- Delivery platform links
- Social media links

## Delivery Process

1. Client information collected
2. Existing template customized
3. Client preview shared
4. Revisions completed
5. Website launched

## Not Included by Default

- Custom delivery system
- Payment processing
- Advanced backend dashboard
- Mobile application
- Domain and third-party service charges

Additional features can be quoted separately.
