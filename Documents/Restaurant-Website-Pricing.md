# Northova Digital — Restaurant Website Pricing

> Internal pricing guide. Final quotation will depend on client requirements.

## Standard Packages

### Starter — $699

- One-page responsive website
- Restaurant introduction
- Featured menu
- Contact details and opening hours
- Google Maps
- Click-to-call and email
- Social media links
- Basic SEO
- Deployment
- One revision round

### Growth — $1,099

Everything in Starter, plus:

- Complete menu presentation
- Gallery and testimonials
- Reservation form or external reservation link
- DoorDash, Uber Eats and ordering links
- Basic analytics setup
- Two revision rounds
- 14 days post-launch support

### Premium — Starting at $1,699

Everything in Growth, plus:

- More customized design
- Additional pages or sections
- OpenTable, Resy or similar integration
- Enhanced local SEO
- Advanced animations
- Three revision rounds
- 30 days post-launch support

## Not Included by Default

- Custom backend
- Payment processing
- Custom ordering or delivery system
- Advanced reservation dashboard
- Domain and third-party platform charges
- Photography, copywriting or branding
- Major changes outside the agreed scope

Additional requirements will be quoted separately.
