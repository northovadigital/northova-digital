# Wang Shack — 3-Point Website Improvement Plan

## 1. Improve the Ordering Transition

Create a smoother journey between the restaurant website and the external ordering platform so customers understand where they are being directed and can continue without confusion.

## 2. Strengthen Calls to Action

Make ordering, reservations, contact details, and location information more prominent so customers can take action quickly from both mobile and desktop devices.

## 3. Add Stronger Social Proof

Highlight customer reviews, food photography, and trust signals more effectively to help new visitors feel confident before placing an order or visiting the restaurant.

## Expected Business Impact

- Fewer customers dropping off during ordering
- More completed customer actions
- Stronger trust with new visitors
- Better overall conversion journey
