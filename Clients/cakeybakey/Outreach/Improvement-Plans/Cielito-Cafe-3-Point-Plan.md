# Cielito Cafe — 3-Point Website Improvement Plan

## 1. Strengthen the Branded Online Presence

Move away from the current Wix subdomain presentation and create a more professional branded website experience that builds stronger trust with new customers.

## 2. Create an Interactive Digital Menu

Replace the external menu-file journey with a structured, mobile-friendly digital menu that is easier to browse and keeps customers within the restaurant’s website.

## 3. Simplify Reservations and Contact Access

Make reservation information, phone details, directions, and contact options more prominent so customers can quickly take action from any device.

## Expected Business Impact

- Stronger brand credibility
- Easier menu browsing for mobile customers
- More direct customer inquiries
- Clearer reservation and contact journey
